Contour Journal is a privacy-focused digital jouranl that, over time, can help you recognize patterns about yourself.

I know, there are a thousand options out there that work the same way, and if you already use one you like, please continue using it, but I think this one is special, and while I may be biased, I have tried to maintain the focus on only building things I would find useful and use, not just building things to have them.

### The Design

Contour Journal is meant to feel cozy. Everything about this is supposed to feel like you're writing in your own personal journal. It is not hyper-optimized to drive you to certain outcomes, it is not supposed to feel overly sleek and sharp or like some fancy product. Is that an excuse for my own inability to design that way? Maybe. But at the same time, I think it fits the intended feeling. The colors are intentionally not very high contrast but should feel soft instead.  

### Privacy

I'll be honest, I'm as guilty as anyone of clicking through every cookie banner without reading it and leaving my location on for apps that probably don't need it. Privacy sounds great until it's inconvenient. So I get it.

Here, privacy isn't an option, it's just how this works. For starters, all personal data (journal entries, actions, routines, thankfuls) are encrypted in the database, basically meaning I can't accidentally read your stuff if I'm in there. There is absolutely no social aspect to this. You cannot share your journal entries short of giving someone your password (please don't), copy/pasting to something else, or taking screenshots. There are no third party cookies, only a single cookie is used so you don't have to login every time you come back. There are _no_ advertizements ever. There is even a blur mode (it's in your profile page) to blur all sensitive text so no one can look over your shoulder to read your info.

Privacy isn't just about the bad guys or the marketers knowing your data. It's much more than that. It's about having your own personal space where no one is competing for your attention to be able to write what needs to be written without _any_ concern of being found out. For me, knowing no one will ever stumble upon the things I write here enable me to be completely honest and candid, which is what makes this whole thing work. 

### Patterns

If you don't use the journal, patterns are a big focus here. In fact, the first page where you login has a tab with several visualizations dedicated to it. There are no specific patterns this journal is trying to show you, but it will present it in a way where you can discover things about yourself. Here are some examples I've found about myself:

- I tend to gravitate towards "fine". It's just who I am. On a scale of 1 to 5, I'm rarely picking 1 or 5. As a result, most of my moods tend to waver right around the middle. That's okay, but it's important for me to know that because it doesn't mean nothing is ever awesome or awful, it just means I don't recognize them as such. I could take that and challenge myself to try to emote bigger, or I could simply recognize that those small fluxuations in what I see from day to day are actually more significant than they look.

- I'm pretty action driven. I like checking the box. It doesn't mean I'll have a better or worse day, it just means I like that structure for my life.

- By time of day, my mood seems to dip when I get off work and rise when my kids are in bed. This doesn't reflect great at surface level, but I think what it's telling me a few things. It could mean I don't write much when I'm with my kids unless I'm feeling not fantastic about something. It could also mean that, after my kids are in bed, I write in reflection of the time I spent with my kids. The entry happens at, say, 8:00 or 9:00, but I'm writing about what happened at 6:00. 

The visualizers tell you what you've given the journal. It's up to you to interpret them based on what you know about yourself. Perhaps they reveal something you'd like to change, or perhaps they just reveal something that feels worth noticeing and understanding about yourself. Either way, I consider it a win!

### Cost

I'm well aware of subscription fatigue, so there are no subscriptions here. I also firmly believe ads have absolutely no place in a personal private journal, so there are no ads here. I will never sell your data. So where does that leave me? 

Well there are a couple of things. First is that there is basically no overhead for this journal. It's not another VC backed product trying to get its investors an ROI. It's just me, and I'm running it with every free option available to me. That has some drawbacks such as occasional down time, but as a user of this journal, I do everything I can to get it back up as quickly as possible. There are also several "modular" features you can pay for to go with the journal. My intent is that the journal is entirely useful and helpful at its base free version and anything paid is just frills, automation, customization, etc. You can pay their low cost _once_ and then you own it and never have to pay for it again. Lastly, I have a [buy me a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/drkgrntt2) that you can visit if you believe in what the journal is doing and want to help support. As soon as it starts making any money, it's going to go toward better-than-free tooling for better maintaining and up time. That said, I want to keep the barrier to entry as low as possible, and I'm not trying to milk you for all you're worth, so if you don't want to pay for anything, don't.
