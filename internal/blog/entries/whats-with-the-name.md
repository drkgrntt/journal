## Contour Journal

Honestly, it's nothing special. There's no incredible origin story about it. I wanted something simple, easy enough to remember, and honestly with an available domain name. The only real requirement was that I didn't hate it. Suffice to say I don't hate it, though I usually feel unsure if I'm even pronouncing it right (con-toor or con-tor).

### The shape of your mind

Not long after deciding the name, I thought the tag line would fit with it and give the name a little more meaning. And to be honest, the more I've used this journal, the more I've found that to be very true to the spirit of the journal. It's not a tool to analyze every entry you write. It's not a storage tool for all your ideas, though you certainly _can_ use bookmarks to retain ideas. It's not about the details, it's about the bigger picture - the shape. As you use the journal, your tendencies can come through and reveal things you never knew about yourself in ways you can either appreciate or work to improve.

---

There's not much else to say about it, but I'm happy with it and I think I landed on the right name. Thanks for reading!
